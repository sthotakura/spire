## XS3463578438

[Pricing supplement PDF](XS3463578438-pricing-supplement.pdf), dated 30 October 2026. [LuxSE viewer](https://www.luxse.com/pdf-viewer/105692613).

- Printed pages 6–7, item 28: Investor Put Event, including partial exercise and at least one Index Business Day's notice before the designated observation date.
- Printed pages 14–15, Schedule 1: index-based early redemption formula and execution cost.
- The standard Investor Put field on page 5 says not applicable; the operative holder right is instead in item 28. Read the custom provisions.
- Offering Circular (No. 1), 12 December 2025, and applicable supplements are incorporated and still need review before modelling the complete contract.

## XS3007576005

[Pricing supplement PDF](XS3007576005-issue-terms.pdf), dated 23 July 2026. [LuxSE viewer](https://www.luxse.com/pdf-viewer/105516177).

Ensemble Investment Corporation SA, Series 2026-12, certificates linked to EQ Gold Overlay Basket.

- Printed pages 5–6, item 24: Investor Put applies, in whole but not in part; valuation follows notice by at least two Basket Business Days, with payment five Basket Business Days after valuation.
- Schedule 1, section 3: Optional Redemption Amount formula. It uses a 10% floor against the sum of an overnight amount and a basket reference amount at put valuation.
- Incorporated General Condition 8(f) and the basket conditions must be read with the issue terms.

## LU2690371641
